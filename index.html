About me:

Hi there! My name is Han! I decided to use this as a chance to make my own personalized recipe page, while I work through The Odin Project! I usually store my recipes on a really long google doc page, so I figured I might as well take this as a chance to create my own organized recipe website!

These recipes are by no means mine or original. A lot of them tend to be picked up on from Youtube videos, online recipe websites and many more. I just make them enough to want to be able to reach back to them in a convenient place.
