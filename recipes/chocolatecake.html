Chocolate Cake

Description

A lovely decadent chocolate cake recipe! I usually make this for family events, or really my own birthdays! A nice thing for this recipe is that it does not use that many dishes so cleaning up afterwards won't take too long!

I stumbed across this comment on a reddit thread, but it mentioned to added boiling water into the mixture, this will help the texture be moist and soft! This makes for a great cake base or even as cake pops! Unfortunately I don't have a proper recipe for chocolate frosting. You may find frostings online or use a premade one at your local grocery store.

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups white sugar
  • 1 3/4 Cups All Purpose Flour
  • 3/4 Unsweetened Cocoa Powder
  • 1 1/12 Tsp Baking Powder
  • 1 1/12 Tsp Baking Soda
  • 1 Tsp Salt
  • 2 Eggs
  • 2 Tsp Vanilla Extract
  • 1 Cup Milk
  • 1/2 Cup BOILING Water

Steps

  1. Mix together flour, cocoa powder. Then add in baking soda and powder, salt, and sugar. Whisk all together.
  2. After whisking, add eggs, milk, vanilla, and oil. Mix all together
  3. Add in boiling water

    4. NOTE: This makes the batter really thin. That's fine and that's what you want. In order to make a moist cake, ya add water. Shocking.

  4. Before pouring batter into your pans, butter and sprinkle the pans with a bit of cocoa powder. Then add in the batter
  5. Have the oven preheated at 350 degrees.
  6. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or do the toothpick method, stick a toothpick into the cake, if it comes out clean walah you made a cake.
